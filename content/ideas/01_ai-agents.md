# AI Agents Are Changing Workflows

- Pillar: AI
- Status: Evergreen / research required
- Audience: General technology audience, students, developers

## Editorial angle
Explain the difference between a chatbot that responds and an agentic system that can plan steps, use tools, and act toward a goal. Avoid implying that agents are fully autonomous or reliable by default.

## Key questions
- What components commonly make up an AI agent?
- How do tool use, memory, and planning differ across implementations?
- What are common failure modes and human oversight requirements?
- Which practical workflows are appropriate for automation?

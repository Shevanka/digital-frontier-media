# AI Hallucinations: When Models Make Things Up

- Pillar: AI
- Status: Evergreen / research required
- Audience: General audience, students, professionals

## Editorial angle
Explain why fluent model output is not equivalent to verified information, and show practical ways to verify important claims.

## Key questions
- What does hallucination mean in generative AI?
- Why can models produce plausible but unsupported answers?
- What roles do retrieval, citations, and evaluation play?
- Which tasks require independent verification?

## Accuracy notes
Avoid suggesting that one technique eliminates hallucinations. Distinguish retrieval support from proof of correctness.

# The Hidden Risks of AI Agents

- Pillar: AI / Cybersecurity
- Status: Evergreen / research required
- Audience: Developers, IT students, technology users

## Editorial angle
Explain how tool permissions, untrusted inputs, prompt injection, excessive autonomy, and weak approval boundaries can create risk in agentic workflows.

## Key questions
- What can go wrong when an agent can access files, browsers, or APIs?
- Why should tool permissions follow least privilege?
- Where should human confirmation be required?
- How can teams log, test, and constrain agent actions?

## Accuracy notes
Use documented examples and defensive recommendations. Do not provide instructions for abusing real services.

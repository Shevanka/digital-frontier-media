## 1. Indonesian Version (ID)

### Hook (00:00 – 00:05)
> "Banyak yang mengira AI agent cuma chatbot pintar biasa. Padahal, ada satu perbedaan mendasar yang mengubah cara kerja kita."

### Narration (00:05 – 00:37)
> "Chatbot hanya menjawab pertanyaan saat kamu tanya. Tapi AI agent punya tiga komponen penting: perencanaan, memori, dan akses ke 'tools' seperti API, database, atau web search. Bayangkan kamu minta 'analisis kompetitor dan kirim laporannya'. AI agent bisa memecah perintah itu jadi 5 langkah kerja, mengeksekusi satu per satu, dan mengecek hasilnya sendiri. Tapi ingat: semakin panjang rantai langkahnya, kemungkinan error juga meningkat. Karena itu, aksi sensitif seperti transfer dana atau hapus data tetap wajib butuh verifikasi manusia."

### Call to Action (00:37 – 00:45)
> "Menurutmu, workflow apa yang paling siap kamu serahkan ke AI agent? Tulis di kolom komentar!"

## 2. English Version (EN)

### Hook (00:00 – 00:05)
> "Most people think AI agents are just fancy chatbots. But one core architectural shift changes everything about how work gets done."

### Narration (00:05 – 00:37)
> "A chatbot simply answers what you ask. An AI agent is fundamentally different: it plans, remembers context, and uses external tools. When you tell an agent to 'analyze five competitors and draft a summary,' it breaks that goal into distinct tasks, calls APIs or browses web data, and evaluates its own progress. But here is the critical caveat: reliability degrades rapidly over long, multi-step chains. That is why human oversight remains non-negotiable for sensitive operations like payment processing or database modifications."

### Call to Action (00:37 – 00:45)
> "What workflow would you trust an AI agent to handle today? Let us know in the comments."

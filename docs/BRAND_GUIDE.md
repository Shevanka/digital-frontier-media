# Brand Guide

## Identity

**Name:** The Digital Frontier  
**Tagline:** Understand Tomorrow, Today.  
**Positioning:** Technology explained simply, accurately, and usefully.  
**Audience:** Indonesia and global English-speaking audiences.

## Editorial promise

Every piece should help the audience understand what changed, how it works, and why it matters. Avoid shallow headline repetition, exaggerated claims, and unnecessary jargon.

## Voice

- Clear, curious, analytical, and approachable.
- Confident when evidence is strong; explicit about uncertainty when it is not.
- Explain technical terms at first use.
- Prefer concrete examples over hype.
- Avoid fearmongering and engagement bait.

## Visual direction

Modern minimalist editorial technology. Use strong typography, restrained color, clean grids, readable diagrams, and purposeful motion. Prioritize legibility on a mobile screen. Do not use decorative effects that compete with the explanation.

## Content pillars

AI 30%; Technology & Innovation 20%; Cybersecurity 25%; Tech News & Internet Culture 15%; Future & Digital Society 10%. Treat the allocation as a starting point and adjust using audience data.

## Language

Maintain separate Indonesian and English adaptations. Hooks may be localized, but factual claims, technical meaning, and caveats must remain consistent.

## Trust requirements

Cite original sources, distinguish fact from analysis, correct material errors transparently, respect privacy, and disclose sponsorships or affiliate relationships.

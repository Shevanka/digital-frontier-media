# Content Strategy

## Editorial framework

Each story should answer four questions: What happened or what is the concept? How does it work? Why does it matter? What should the audience understand or do next?

## Pillar allocation

- AI: 30%
- Technology & Innovation: 20%
- Cybersecurity: 25%
- Tech News & Internet Culture: 15%
- Future & Digital Society: 10%

## Repeatable series

- AI Explained: concepts, agents, models, limitations, and practical implications.
- Security in 40 Seconds: vulnerabilities, defensive controls, privacy, and secure development.
- The Tech Brief: verified developments with context and implications.
- Internet Culture, Explained: platforms, online behavior, and emerging digital norms.
- Future Signals: robotics, infrastructure, digital rights, and societal change.

## Format selection

Use a quick explainer for one concept, story-led format for a mechanism or consequence, news brief for dated developments, myth-vs-fact for misconceptions, and carousel for saveable reference material.

## Topic selection

Score candidate topics on audience relevance, evidence quality, explanatory value, timeliness, visual potential, and production effort. Do not treat a high trend score as proof of audience demand.

## Cadence

Start with 3–5 master videos per week. Pilot five topics first, review performance and production time, then adjust. Repurpose validated topics into carousels and longer explainers.

## Measurement

Track reach, average watch time, completion, shares, saves, comments, follower conversion, production time, and source completeness. Compare formats and topic clusters over a meaningful sample.

# Platform Strategy

## TikTok

Primary discovery and hook-testing channel. Lead with a clear question, surprising but accurate fact, or relatable problem. Keep captions concise and invite substantive discussion.

## Instagram Reels

Prioritize visual identity, shareability, and saves. Use readable on-screen text, consistent cover design, and captions that add context rather than repeat narration.

## YouTube Shorts

Optimize the title for clear topic discovery. Deliver the central explanation quickly and use the description for source references and additional context where appropriate.

## Cross-platform rules

Do not assume one export performs equally everywhere. Adapt title, caption, cover, opening frame, and call to action. Preserve factual claims and caveats across versions. Review platform-specific policies and current format limits before publishing.

## Secondary formats

Use Instagram carousels for structured explainers, YouTube long-form for validated topics requiring depth, and a newsletter or website for owned distribution once the publishing cadence is sustainable.

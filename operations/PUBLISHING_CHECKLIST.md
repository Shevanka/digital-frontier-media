# Publishing Checklist

## Editorial
- [ ] Research brief approved
- [ ] Every material claim checked against original sources
- [ ] Dates, versions, numbers, and names rechecked
- [ ] Indonesian and English factual parity confirmed
- [ ] No misleading hook, unsupported urgency, or omitted caveat

## Production
- [ ] Narration and pronunciation reviewed
- [ ] Subtitles synchronized and legible
- [ ] Visuals clarify the explanation
- [ ] Footage, images, and audio rights confirmed
- [ ] Personal data and sensitive information protected
- [ ] Export reviewed on a mobile screen

## Platform
- [ ] Correct title, caption, hashtags, and cover
- [ ] Description/source links checked where applicable
- [ ] Platform-specific policy and format requirements checked
- [ ] Human approval recorded
- [ ] Published URL and date recorded in tracker

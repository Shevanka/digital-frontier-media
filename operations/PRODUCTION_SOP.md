# Production SOP

## 1. Select

Choose an evergreen backlog item or a dated trend lead. Confirm that the topic fits an editorial pillar and has a useful explanatory angle.

## 2. Research

Open original sources. Record dates, claims, evidence, caveats, and uncertainty in the canonical research brief. Treat RSS and AI summaries as discovery aids only.

## 3. Draft

Generate or write a master narrative. Keep one central idea, a clear hook, concise explanation, and useful implication. Mark unsupported claims for verification.

## 4. Adapt

Create Indonesian and English versions from the same approved facts. Localize phrasing, not factual scope. Prepare platform-specific titles, captions, hashtags, and calls to action.

## 5. Storyboard

Plan a vertical 9:16 sequence. Every visual should clarify the narration. Specify overlays, footage or graphics, timing, and asset requirements.

## 6. Produce

Record or synthesize narration, create subtitles, source licensed visuals and audio, edit, and export according to current platform specifications.

## 7. Review

Verify every claim and source, language parity, pronunciation, subtitle accuracy, asset rights, privacy, audio levels, visual legibility, and final export. Record human approval.

## 8. Publish and measure

Adapt metadata per platform. Publish manually after approval. Record URLs and performance metrics in the content tracker. Review results weekly and document lessons.

## Non-negotiable

No generated draft is publication-ready by default. No automatic social publishing is enabled.

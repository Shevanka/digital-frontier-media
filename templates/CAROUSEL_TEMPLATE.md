# Carousel Template

- Topic:
- Pillar:
- Language:
- Target: 5–8 slides
- Status: DRAFT

1. **Cover:** clear topic and useful promise.
2. **Context:** why the topic matters.
3. **Mechanism:** how it works.
4. **Example:** concrete, sourced illustration.
5. **Implication:** what changes or what is at risk.
6. **Takeaway:** what the audience should remember.
7. **Sources:** primary references and dates.
8. **CTA:** invite a relevant save, share, or discussion.

Keep one main idea per slide. Use readable mobile typography and cite factual claims.

# Research and Fact-Check Policy

## Core rule

Accuracy takes priority over speed. Trend feeds, search snippets, headlines, and AI summaries are leads only. They are not sufficient evidence for publication.

## Source hierarchy

1. Primary sources: official advisories, standards, documentation, original research, court or regulatory records, company disclosures, and direct statements.
2. Institutional sources: recognized security agencies, standards bodies, universities, and established research organizations.
3. Reputable secondary reporting: use for context and independent corroboration.
4. Social posts, forums, and aggregators: useful for discovery or attributed reaction, not sole support for consequential claims.

## Claim register

Record each material claim, source URL, source publication date, date accessed, exact supporting passage or data, and status:
- `VERIFIED`
- `NEEDS_VERIFICATION`
- `CONTEXT_ONLY`

Do not narrate claims marked `NEEDS_VERIFICATION`. Remove them or verify them first.

## Time-sensitive claims

Recheck current status, dates, versions, affected products, incident scope, and figures immediately before publication. Distinguish announcement date from release date and discovery date from disclosure date.

## Cybersecurity coverage

Use authorized labs and defensive framing. Avoid enabling unauthorized access, credential theft, persistence, or exploitation against real targets. Explain impact, root cause, mitigations, and responsible disclosure.

## AI coverage

Distinguish demonstrated capability from marketing claims. Note model/version, benchmark conditions, limitations, and relevant uncertainty. Do not infer general capability from a single demo.

## Corrections and rights

Correct material errors transparently. Respect privacy, copyright, attribution, licenses, and platform policies. Confirm permission for footage, music, images, and datasets.

## Publication gate

A human editor must verify sources, claims, translation parity, subtitles, pronunciation, asset rights, privacy, and final export. AI output must never auto-publish.
